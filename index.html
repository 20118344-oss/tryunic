FAQ

Common
questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Drop us a message through the booking form and we'll get back to you.

We specialise in gel manicures, BIAB (Builder in a Bottle), acrylic nail extensions, nail art, spa pedicures, and Shellac. We also offer seasonal collections and bespoke nail art — ask us at booking.

A gel manicure takes approximately 60–75 minutes. A full acrylic set with nail art can take up to 2–2.5 hours. We'll always give you a clear time estimate when you book so you can plan your day.

We strongly recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time and technician. We do our best to accommodate walk-ins when availability allows, but same-day spots can be limited, especially on weekends.

We work exclusively with professional-grade products — CND Shellac, OPI, Gelish, and premium Japanese gel brands. All products we use are cruelty-free and formulated to be kind to your nails and skin.

Absolutely. Our technicians are trained in a wide range of techniques — from clean minimalist linework and negative space to intricate 3D floral and hand-painted art. Bring your inspiration and we'll create something personal to you.

We're based in Wexford, Ireland. Our studio is open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00am – 7:00pm, and Sunday 11:00am – 5:00pm. We're closed on Mondays. Exact address will be confirmed when you book.
